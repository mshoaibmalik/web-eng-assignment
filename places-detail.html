Mohatta Palace

Pink stone, cool corridors and a century of changing Karachi.

A palace with many lives

Built in 1927 by businessman Shivratan Chandraratan Mohatta, this Indo-Saracenic residence was designed by architect Agha Ahmed Hussain. Its pink Jodhpur stone and yellow Gizri stone details were intended to keep the building cool in Karachi's heat.

After Partition, the palace became associated with the story of Fatima Jinnah. Today it is a museum where exhibitions, craft and architecture meet beneath a skyline of domes.
