Craft

Sindhi Ajrak

Block-printed cotton shawls use deep indigo, madder red and geometric motifs to carry generations of Sindh's visual language.

Craft

Hala pottery

Turquoise and cobalt ceramics from Hala bring cool colour and hand-painted rhythm to everyday life.

Craft

Camel-skin lamps

Delicate cutwork turns translucent camel hide into warm lampshades inspired by local flora.
