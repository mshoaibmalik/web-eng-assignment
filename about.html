A living timeline

Where Karachi came from

The city's history is a layered story of fishing villages, a busy colonial port and a global megacity.

Four turning points

Each era left behind places, traditions and communities that still shape the city.

1729

Kolachi-jo-Goth

A small fishing settlement grew around a protected harbour, named after the fisherwoman Mai Kolachi in local tradition.

1843

Port city begins

After the British annexation of Sindh, roads, railways, civic buildings and the Port Trust transformed Karachi into a trading gateway.

1947

A new capital

Migration after Pakistan's independence changed Karachi's language, cuisine, neighbourhoods and cultural imagination.

2000s–present

A global megacity

Commerce, technology, art and an expanding coastline now meet in a city that never stops reinventing itself.
