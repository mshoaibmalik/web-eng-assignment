Eat like a local

The city is served

Karachi's culinary culture is a delicious map of migration, memory and late-night energy.

Burns Road

Spice and tradition

Try biryani, nihari, haleem and rabri among the original food streets.

Must try: Student Biryani, Waheed Kabab

Boat Basin

All-night comfort

Seekh kebabs, paratha rolls and chai make this a favourite after-dark stop.

Typical plate: PKR 350–900

Do Darya

Dinner by the sea

Seafood and Pakistani favourites served with a view of the Arabian Sea.

Best hour: Sunset

Food safety note

Choose busy stalls with high turnover, carry hand sanitiser and drink sealed or filtered water. Go early for the freshest dishes and avoid carrying valuables in crowded lanes.
